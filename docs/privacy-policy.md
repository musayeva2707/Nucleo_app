# Nucleo — Privacy Policy

Nucleo does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

- The app has no internet permission, no accounts, no ads and no analytics.
- Your progress is saved only on your device.
- Exported save files are created only when you choose to export them, and you control where they go.
- Your phone's own system backup (Android Auto Backup) may include the save file, as with any app. Nucleo itself sends nothing.
